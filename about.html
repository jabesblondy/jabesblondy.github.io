Jabes Blondy is an emerging business and institutional leader, entrepreneur and youth development advocate whose interests span business strategy, organisational governance, innovation, technology, entrepreneurship and African development.

He is the Founder and Executive President of AYLA Africa, a youth-led Pan-African organisation advancing leadership development, innovation, digital skills, entrepreneurship and sustainable development. He is also the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of NextStep Student Network (NSSN International), a youth-focused student empowerment and transition-support initiative connecting young people with information, guidance, skills and opportunities across educational, career and personal development transitions.

Jabes is pursuing a Bachelor of Commerce at Kenyatta University. His professional outlook combines institutional leadership, enterprise thinking and a commitment to practical solutions that strengthen people and organisations.
