Transformative Leader · Keynote Speaker · Tech & Social Entrepreneur · Innovation, Business & Enterprise

Jabes Blondy is a Kenyan transformative leader, entrepreneur, institution builder and emerging speaker whose interests and work span business, leadership, entrepreneurship, technology, innovation, organisational development, youth empowerment and Pan-African development. He is interested in architecting systems, strengthening institutions, advancing ideas and helping unlock opportunity in a changing global landscape.

He is the Founder and Executive President of AYLA Africa, a youth-led Pan-African organisation advancing leadership development, innovation, digital skills, entrepreneurship, collaboration and sustainable development. He is also the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of NextStep Student Network (NSSN International), a youth-focused student empowerment and transition-support initiative with a global outlook.

Jabes is pursuing a Bachelor of Commerce at Kenyatta University. His developing leadership portfolio includes student representation, elected chapter leadership, committee service, institutional engagement and ambassadorships. He is committed to learning, building, collaborating and documenting his journey as he develops ideas and institutions.

His perspectives are grounded in curiosity, purposeful ambition, stewardship, collaboration and the conviction that durable progress depends on capable people, strong institutions and practical execution.
