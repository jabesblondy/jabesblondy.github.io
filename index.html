Jabes Blondy is a Kenyan entrepreneur, transformative leader and Pan-Africanist. He is the Founder of AYLA Africa, advancing youth leadership, innovation and enterprise across the continent.

Driven by Africapitalism, he believes in the power of entrepreneurship, technology and responsible leadership to unlock Africa's potential and create lasting impact.
